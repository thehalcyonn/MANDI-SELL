How Government Data Integration Works:

MandiSell's backend architecture is designed to interface with the Open Government Data (OGD) Platform India (Data.gov.in Agmarknet API) and e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) feeds. For hackathon testing and demo stability, realistic benchmark distributions are pre-seeded in SQLite to ensure 100% uptime without external rate-limiting.
